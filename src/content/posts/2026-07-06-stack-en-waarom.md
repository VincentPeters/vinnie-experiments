The stack: Astro on Cloudflare Pages for the blog itself, Polars and
scikit-learn and statsmodels for analysis, Jupyter in VS Code for the
notebook surface, Pyodide in the browser for the rare interactive
demo. One choice per layer, frozen for 12 months.

The argument: every change to this list is one less hour spent on
methodology, statistics, or writing. Tool sprawl is the enemy.
